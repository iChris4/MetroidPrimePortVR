# Licence provenance

Keep licence texts verbatim. Do not replace component licences with the root
MIT grant or with short source-file attribution summaries.

The `remastered-*` notices are restored from the original notices distributed
with PrimedGun's Remastered importer. Their original projects are:

- tegra_swizzle 0.3.2: https://github.com/ScanMountGoat/tegra_swizzle/tree/v0.3.2
- bcdec_rs 0.1.2: https://github.com/ScanMountGoat/image_dds
- bcdec: https://github.com/iOrange/bcdec
- astc-decode 0.3.1: https://crates.io/crates/astc-decode/0.3.1
- retrotool: https://github.com/encounter/retrotool
- Ryujinx: the original Ryujinx Team and Contributors MIT notice.

The tegra_swizzle and bcdec_rs texts were also checked byte-for-byte against
their original project licence files. The astc-decode notice preserves both
Weiyi Wang's and UNC Chapel Hill's attribution. Its Apache terms are included
in full in `Apache-2.0.txt`.

The full GPL v3 text is retained at the repository root in `COPYING`, and
packaged as `GPL-3.0.txt` for files marked GPL-3.0-or-later. Upstream's MIT grant is preserved verbatim in `native-port-MIT.txt`. Aurora's
and MusyX's MIT licences remain unchanged.
